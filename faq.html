Order Basics订单基础问题

Questions buyers usually ask before quotation.报价前客户通常会问的问题。

Q1

What products do you mainly make?你们主要做什么产品？

We focus on streetwear menswear, especially varsity jackets, hoodies, heavyweight T-shirts, outerwear and pants.我们以街头男装为主，尤其擅长棒球服、卫衣、重磅 T 恤、夹克外套和裤装。

Q2

Can you make from photos or samples?可以来图来样吗？

Yes. Photos, original samples, tech packs and artwork can be used for project review.可以。图片、实物样、工艺单和图案文件都可用于项目评估。

Q3

What is the MOQ?起订量是多少？

200 pcs can be reviewed by product, fabric, craft complexity, color count and schedule. 500-1000 pcs brand orders are a stronger fit.200 件可按品类、面料、工艺复杂度、颜色数量和排期评估；500-1000 件品牌单更适合长期合作。

Q4

Can you support repeat runs?可以做返单吗？

Yes. We have handled larger programs including a 10,000 pcs fleece hoodie order and orders up to about 50,000 pcs. Final planning depends on material and schedule.可以。我们做过 10000 件抓绒卫衣项目，也承接过约 50000 件规模订单。最终需按物料和排期规划。

Q5

How is price confirmed?价格怎么确认？

Price depends on style structure, fabric, trims, craft, quantity, packing and delivery target.价格取决于款式结构、面辅料、工艺、数量、包装和目标交期。

Q6

What should I send first?我应该先发什么？

Send product category, reference photos, target quantity, fabric idea or GSM, artwork, craft requirements, size range, delivery deadline and shipping country.可先发产品品类、参考图、目标数量、面料想法或克重、图案文件、工艺要求、尺码段、目标交期和收货国家。

Production & Quality生产与质量

Questions that affect sample and bulk production.影响样衣和大货的问题。

Q7

How do you control quality?质量怎么控制？

We follow approved samples, written order notes, measurement checks, workmanship review and packing confirmation.按确认样、书面订单备注、尺寸检查、做工复核和包装确认执行。

Q8

Can you handle complex craft?复杂工艺能做吗？

Chenille embroidery, patch embroidery, panel construction, rib trims, washing, distressing, heavyweight fabrics, rubber print, garment dye and digital print can be reviewed by sample.毛巾绣、贴布绣、拼接、罗纹、水洗、破坏、重磅面料、胶印、成衣染和数码印都可按样衣评估。

Q9

Do you provide certificates?可以提供证书资料吗？

Available documents should be confirmed by project. We only show verified materials.可提供的资料需按项目确认，我们只展示已核实材料。

Q10

How long does sampling and bulk production take?打样和大货一般多久？

Sampling usually takes 3-7 days. Bulk production is often 10-15 days after sample, material, craft and schedule are confirmed.常规打样 3-7 天；样衣、面辅料、工艺和排期确认后，大货通常 10-15 天左右。
