Fabric test first先看面料

Effect sample效果样确认

Color shade, hand feel, shrinkage and distressing level should be checked by sample.颜色深浅、手感、缩水率和做旧程度建议按效果样确认。

03

Bulk control大货控制

Bulk washing should follow approved sample, production notes and acceptable shade range.大货水洗应按确认样、生产备注和可接受色差范围执行。
