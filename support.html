FAQ

Common questions before the first order.首次合作前常见问题。

Open full FAQ打开完整FAQ >
Q1

What products do you mainly make?你们主要做什么产品？

Streetwear menswear, especially varsity jackets, hoodies, heavyweight T-shirts, outerwear and pants.主要是街头男装，尤其是棒球服、卫衣、重磅 T 恤、夹克外套和裤装。

Q2

Can you make from photos or samples?可以来图来样吗？

Yes. Photos, original samples, tech packs and artwork can be used for project review.可以，图片、实物样衣、工艺单和图案文件都可以用于评估。

Q3

What order size is a good fit?什么数量更适合？

200 pcs can be reviewed, while 500-1000 pcs brand orders and larger repeat programs are a stronger fit.200 件可评估；500-1000 件品牌单和更大数量返单更适合长期合作。

Q4

Can you support private label packing?可以做贴牌包装吗？

Labels, hangtags, polybags, carton marks and packing details can be confirmed by order.主唛、吊牌、胶袋、箱唛和包装细节可按订单确认。

Q5

How is pricing confirmed?价格怎么确认？

Pricing depends on style, material, craft, quantity, packing and production schedule.价格取决于款式、面料、工艺、数量、包装和生产排期。

Q6

How do you control quality?质量怎么控制？

We use approved samples, measurement checks, workmanship review and packing confirmation.通过封样、尺寸检查、做工检查和包装确认来把控。
