Size & Fit Guide尺码版型指南

Clear size and fit confirmation reduces production risk.尺码和版型确认清楚，可以降低生产风险。

For custom orders, measurements should be confirmed with the same measuring method, tolerance and approved sample.定制订单建议统一测量方法、公差范围，并以确认样为生产参考。

Size chart尺码表

Provide chest, shoulder, length, sleeve, waist, hip and key measurements by style.按款式提供胸围、肩宽、衣长、袖长、腰围、臀围等关键尺寸。

02

Fit direction版型方向

Confirm oversized, regular, slim, cropped, relaxed or teamwear fit direction before sampling.打样前确认宽松、常规、修身、短款、休闲或队服版型方向。

03

Tolerance公差范围

Tolerance should be agreed by category, fabric behavior and production method.公差应根据品类、面料特性和生产方式确认。
