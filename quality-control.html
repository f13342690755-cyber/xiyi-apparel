Quality Control Statement质量控制声明

Quality is controlled by approved samples, written standards and production checks.质量通过确认样、书面标准和生产检查共同控制。

For every custom apparel order, final standards should be confirmed by actual style, fabric, craft, quantity and order requirements.每个定制服装订单的最终标准，都应根据实际款式、面料、工艺、数量和订单要求确认。

Our Position我们的质量立场

We do not treat quality as a slogan. We turn it into checkpoints.质量不是一句口号，而是每个环节的确认点。

Xiyi Apparel manages quality through project review, sample approval, material confirmation, inline checks, measurement review, final inspection and packing confirmation. We avoid unconfirmed claims and communicate order standards before bulk production.喜艺服饰通过项目评估、样衣确认、面辅料确认、中期检查、尺寸复核、尾查和包装确认来管理质量。我们不做未经确认的承诺，而是在大货前把订单标准沟通清楚。

Control Points质量控制点

Key checks before and during production.生产前和生产中的关键检查。

01

Project review项目评估

Review style structure, fabric direction, craft complexity, quantity and delivery target before quotation.报价前评估款式结构、面料方向、工艺复杂度、数量和交期目标。

02

Approved sample确认样标准

Bulk production should follow the approved sample, sample comments and written production notes.大货应按照确认样、样衣修改意见和书面生产备注执行。

03

Material confirmation面辅料确认

Fabric, color, trims, labels, zippers, rib, drawcords and packing materials should be confirmed before production.面料、颜色、辅料、标签、拉链、罗纹、抽绳和包装物料应在生产前确认。

04

Craft placement check工艺位置检查

Embroidery, printing, patches, numbers, logos and special surface details are checked by artwork and sample standard.刺绣、印花、章仔、号码、Logo和特殊表面细节按图案文件和样衣标准检查。

05

Measurement review尺寸复核

Measurement method, key points and tolerance should be confirmed with the buyer before bulk checking.尺寸测量方法、重点部位和公差范围应在大货检查前与客户确认。

06

Final packing check尾查与包装确认

Before handoff, we check workmanship, thread trimming, labels, folding, polybags, carton marks and packing quantity.交货前检查做工、剪线、标签、折叠、胶袋、箱唛和装箱数量。

Buyer Confirmation客户确认责任

Clear buyer confirmation helps reduce production risk.客户资料确认清楚，生产风险会更低。

A

Confirm size chart, measuring method and tolerance before bulk production.大货前确认尺码表、测量方法和公差范围。

B

Confirm fabric, color, craft artwork, logo placement, labels and packing method.确认面料、颜色、工艺图案、Logo位置、标签和包装方式。

C

Keep key changes in writing so the production team can follow the latest approved standard.关键修改建议保留文字记录，方便生产团队按最新确认标准执行。

Issue Handling问题处理方式

If a quality concern appears, we review it by evidence and order standard.如出现质量疑问，按证据和订单标准一起复核。

When a concern is found before shipment, we communicate with photos, videos or inspection notes and discuss a correction plan where practical. After delivery, any quality discussion should be reviewed against the approved sample, written order details, inspection standard and shipping handoff records.出货前如发现问题，可通过照片、视频或检验记录沟通，并在可执行范围内讨论修正方案。到货后的质量沟通，应结合确认样、书面订单细节、检验标准和出货交接记录进行复核。

01

Evidence first先看证据

Photos, videos, measurements and carton information help locate the issue.照片、视频、尺寸数据和箱唛信息有助于定位问题。

02

Compare standard对照标准

Compare with approved sample, tech pack, tolerance and written comments.对照确认样、工艺单、公差范围和文字修改意见。

03

Discuss solution沟通方案

Correction, sorting or next-order prevention can be discussed by case.可按实际情况沟通修正、分拣或下次订单预防方案。
