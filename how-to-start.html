Start Point起步资料

Send what you already have.先把你现有的资料发来。

A photo, original sample, drawing, size chart, logo artwork or rough idea can all start the discussion. We will tell you what still needs to be confirmed.图片、原样、设计图、尺码表、Logo文件或大概想法都可以先发来，我们会告诉你还需要补充什么。

01

Reference photos参考图片

Front, back, detail and fit references help us judge construction.正面、背面、细节和上身效果图有助于判断结构。

02

Physical sample实物样衣

Original samples make fabric, workmanship and fit discussion more accurate.实物样衣能让面料、做工和版型沟通更准确。

03

Tech pack工艺单

Measurement chart, construction notes, artwork placement and packing requirements are useful.尺码表、结构说明、图案位置和包装要求都很有帮助。

Cooperation Flow合作流程

A clear route from review to bulk order.从评估到大货的清晰路径。

01

Project review项目评估

Review style, fabric, craft, quantity, target price and delivery requirements.评估款式、面料、工艺、数量、目标价格和交期。

02

Quotation direction报价方向

Confirm the information needed before a formal quotation or sample fee.确认正式报价或打样费用前需要补齐的信息。

03

Sample development样衣开发

Fit, measurement, fabric, craft and comments are adjusted around the sample.围绕样衣调整版型、尺寸、面料、工艺和修改意见。

04

Pre-production confirmation产前确认

Confirm approved sample, material, artwork, labels, packing and size ratio.确认封样、面辅料、图案、标签、包装和尺码配比。

05

Bulk production大货生产

Cutting, craft, sewing, finishing and packing follow the approved standard.裁剪、工艺、车缝、整烫和包装按确认标准推进。

06

Shipment handoff出货交接

Carton details, packing records and delivery arrangement are confirmed before handoff.出货前确认箱规、包装记录和交接安排。
