Extended Size Grading加大码与放码

Size expansion needs grading rules, fit checks and tolerance control.尺码扩展需要放码规则、版型检查和公差控制。

For brand orders, the size range should be confirmed by category, fabric stretch, fit direction and target market size chart.品牌订单的尺码段，需要按品类、面料弹性、版型方向和目标尺码表确认。

01

Base size基础尺码

Confirm the sample size first, then build smaller and larger sizes from it.先确认样衣基础码，再根据基础码扩展大小尺码。

02

Grading interval放码档差

Chest, shoulder, length, waist, hip and sleeve increments should be written clearly.胸围、肩宽、衣长、腰围、臀围、袖长等档差需要写清楚。

03

Fit review版型复核

Large sizes may need adjusted armhole, rise, cuff, pocket and length proportions.大码可能需要调整袖窿、裆深、脚口、口袋和长度比例。

04

Tolerance standard公差标准

Tolerance should be confirmed by fabric, washing, category and measurement method.公差需根据面料、水洗、品类和测量方法确认。

05

Size ratio尺码配比

Bulk production should include final quantity for every color and size.大货生产需要确认每个颜色和尺码的最终数量。

06

What to provide需要提供

Send your size chart, sample size, target range and key fit notes.请提供尺码表、样衣码、目标尺码段和关键版型要求。
