Sample Development样衣开发

3-7 days sampling from photo, sample or tech pack.从图片、样衣或工艺单开始，3-7 天打样。

Sampling helps confirm whether the style can be produced smoothly in bulk, whether the craft route is stable, and whether the cost direction matches the buyer's target.打样不只是做出外观，还要确认款式能否顺利大货、工艺路线是否稳定、成本方向是否符合客户目标。

Home / Sample Development
01

Review what the buyer sends评估客户现有资料

Photos, original samples, size charts, artwork, target quantity and delivery target are reviewed first.先评估图片、原样、尺码表、图案文件、目标数量和交期要求。

02

Suggest fabric and craft options提供面料和工艺建议

We help choose fabric, trims, embroidery, print, wash, patch and finishing routes that fit the style and budget.根据款式和预算，协助选择面料、辅料、刺绣、印花、水洗、贴布和后整路线。

03

Make sample and collect comments制作样衣并记录修改意见

Fit, measurements, hand feel, craft placement and workmanship comments are recorded before revisions or bulk approval.版型、尺寸、手感、工艺位置和做工意见会在改样或大货确认前记录清楚。

04

Lock bulk production standard锁定大货生产标准

The approved sample, material list, artwork notes, labels, packing and QC points become the basis for bulk production.确认样、面辅料清单、图案备注、标签、包装和质检重点，会作为大货生产依据。
