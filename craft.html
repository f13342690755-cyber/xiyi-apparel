How craft is confirmed工艺怎么确认

Artwork, fabric, budget and bulk stability decide the craft route.图案、面料、预算和大货稳定性共同决定工艺路线。

01

Confirm artwork placement, size, color, file format and target visual effect.确认图案位置、尺寸、颜色、文件格式和目标视觉效果。

02

Review whether embroidery, print, wash, patch or distressing suits the selected fabric and garment construction.评估刺绣、印花、水洗、贴布或破坏工艺是否适合当前面料和衣服结构。

03

Use sampling comments to balance look, cost, hand feel and repeatable bulk production quality.通过样衣意见平衡外观效果、成本、手感和大货稳定性。
