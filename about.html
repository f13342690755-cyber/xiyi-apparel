Company Profile公司简介

Dongguan Xiyi Apparel Co., Ltd.东莞市喜艺服饰有限公司

Dongguan Xiyi Apparel Co., Ltd. is a men's streetwear OEM/ODM factory founded on January 14, 2011 in Humen, Dongguan. We focus on practical custom development for hoodies, heavyweight T-shirts, varsity jackets, outerwear and pants.东莞市喜艺服饰有限公司于 2011 年 1 月 14 日成立，位于广东东莞虎门，专注卫衣、重磅 T 恤、棒球服、夹克外套和裤装等街头男装 OEM/ODM 定制开发。

Most buyers come to us with a reference photo, original sample or tech pack. Our team reviews the style, fabric, fit, craft route, cost direction and production feasibility, then supports sampling, comments, bulk production and repeat orders.多数客户会先提供参考图、实物样衣或工艺单。我们会评估款式、面料、版型、工艺路线、成本方向和生产可行性，再配合打样、改样、大货和返单。

Our current factory setup includes 100+ team members, 63 sewing stations, 8 cutting tables, 10 finishing staff and 20 quality-control staff. This structure is a good match for 500-1000 pcs brand orders, larger repeat programs and trading-company bulk orders.目前工厂团队 100 多人，配置 63 个车位、8 张裁床、10 名后整人员和 20 名质检人员，适合 500-1000 件品牌单、更大数量返单和贸易公司大货订单。
