Company Profile公司简介

Dongguan Xiyi Apparel Co., Ltd.东莞市喜艺服饰有限公司

Xiyi Apparel is located in Humen, Dongguan. The factory supports men's apparel OEM/ODM, CMT manufacturing, bulk customization, private-label production and FOB order discussion.喜艺服饰位于广东东莞虎门，承接男装 OEM/ODM、CMT 来料加工、批量定制、品牌贴牌和外贸 FOB 订单沟通。
