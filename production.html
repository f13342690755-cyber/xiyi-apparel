Production Flow生产流程

The working path buyers need to understand.采购客户需要看懂的生产路径。

01

Project review项目评估

Style, quantity, material direction and delivery target.款式、数量、面料方向和交期目标。

02

Pattern and sample纸样与样衣

Fit, measurement, construction and sample comments.版型、尺寸、结构和样衣修改意见。

03

Fabric and trims面辅料确认

Fabric, rib, zipper, drawcord, labels and packing items.面料、罗纹、拉链、抽绳、标签和包装物料。

04

Cutting裁剪

Cutting by confirmed fabric direction, size ratio and order notes.按照确认面料方向、尺码配比和订单备注裁剪。

05

Craft process工艺加工

Printing, embroidery, patch, wash or special surface process.印花、刺绣、章仔、水洗或特殊表面工艺。

06

Sewing车缝

Construction follows approved sample and workmanship standard.按确认样和做工标准进行车缝。

07

Inline checks中期检查

Check measurements, defects, craft placement and key details.检查尺寸、疵点、工艺位置和关键细节。

08

Finishing后整

Thread trimming, pressing, folding, labels and hangtags.剪线、整烫、折叠、标签和吊牌。

09

Packing包装

Polybags, carton marks, quantity per carton and packing photos.胶袋、箱唛、装箱数量和包装记录。

Quality Control质量控制
