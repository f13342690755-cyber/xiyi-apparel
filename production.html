Production Flow生产流程

A clear working path from reference image to shipment-ready goods.从参考图到可出货大货的清晰路径。

01

Reference review参考资料评估

Photos, samples or tech packs are reviewed for style, fit, fabric, craft and quantity.根据图片、样衣或工艺单评估款式、版型、面料、工艺和数量。

02

Material and craft route面料和工艺路线

We suggest practical options for fabric, trims, embroidery, print, wash, patch and cost control.提供面料、辅料、刺绣、印花、水洗、贴布和成本控制方面的可行建议。

03

Pattern order下样板单

Pattern direction is arranged after key measurements, fit direction and construction details are confirmed.确认关键尺寸、版型方向和结构细节后安排下样板单。

04

Sample making样衣制作

The first sample is used to check shape, workmanship, craft placement, fabric hand feel and cost direction.首件样衣用于确认廓形、做工、工艺位置、面料手感和成本方向。

05

Sample comments改样意见

Buyer comments are recorded clearly before revisions, pre-production sample or bulk standard approval.客户修改意见会记录清楚，再进入改样、产前样或大货标准确认。

06

Bulk preparation大货准备

Confirm fabric, trims, color, size ratio, labels, packing, delivery plan and inspection points.确认面料、辅料、颜色、尺码配比、标签、包装、交期和检验重点。

07

Cutting and sewing裁剪和车缝

Cutting and sewing follow the approved sample, order notes and workmanship standard.裁剪和车缝按确认样、订单备注和做工标准执行。

08

QC and finishing质检和后整

Measurements, defects, craft placement, labels, thread trimming, pressing and folding are checked.检查尺寸、疵点、工艺位置、标签、剪线、整烫和折叠。

09

Packing and handoff包装和交接

Polybags, carton marks, quantity per carton, packing photos and shipment handoff are confirmed.确认胶袋、箱唛、装箱数量、包装记录和出货交接。

Quality Control质量控制

Quality is managed by approved samples, written details and in-process checks.品质靠确认样、文字细节和过程检查共同管理。

Before bulk production, we confirm measurement tolerance, workmanship standard, artwork placement, labels, packing method and inspection points. During production, QC staff check key details so the bulk order follows the approved sample.大货前确认尺寸公差、做工标准、图案位置、标签、包装方式和检验重点；生产过程中由质检人员检查关键细节，确保大货跟随确认样标准。
